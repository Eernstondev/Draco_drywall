Services

Drywall installation, finishing and repair for residential and commercial projects.

Drywall Installation

Hanging and fastening drywall on wood or steel framing for new builds, additions and renovations. Board is laid out to keep joints to a minimum and cut cleanly around openings, outlets and fixtures.

  • Standard, moisture-resistant and fire-rated board
  • Walls and partitions on wood or steel studs
  • Basements, additions and full renovations

Taping & Finishing

Taping, coating and sanding to the finish level your project requires, from fire-taping in service areas to a full skim coat on walls under strong or grazing light.

  • Tape and compound on all joints and fasteners
  • Corner bead and trims
  • Finish levels matched to paint and lighting

Drywall Repair

Patching holes, cracks, water damage and popped fasteners, blended into the existing surface so the repair disappears after paint.

  • Holes and impact damage
  • Cracks and nail pops
  • Water-damaged sections cut out and replaced

Ceiling Installation

Flat, sloped and tray ceilings, plus suspended and furred ceilings where height, services or acoustics call for them.

  • Flat, sloped and tray ceilings
  • Suspended and furred ceilings
  • Bulkheads and soffits

Residential Drywall

New homes, basements, renovations and additions. We protect floors and finishes, keep dust contained and leave the site clean at the end of each day.

  • Basements and additions
  • Renovations in occupied homes
  • New residential construction

Commercial Drywall

Offices, retail units and tenant improvements, with fire-rated and acoustic assemblies built to the project drawings and coordinated with the other trades on site.

  • Steel stud partitions and demising walls
  • Fire-rated and acoustic assemblies
  • Tenant improvements and fit-outs

How a project runs

The same four steps for a single repair or a full commercial fit-out.

  1. Request a quote

    Send the project details and photos through the quote form.

  2. Site visit

    We measure, confirm the scope and the finish level, and discuss timing.

  3. Written quote

    You receive a quote listing the scope, materials and schedule.

  4. Work and walkthrough

    We complete the work and review the finished surfaces with you.
