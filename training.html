Drywall Training

Hands-on courses in drywall installation, taping and finishing, from the first sheet to the final coat.

Learn the trade on real walls and ceilings, not from slides.

Each course is practical. You work with the same tools, board and compound used on site, on full-size assemblies, with an instructor checking your work as you go.

Courses suit people starting in the trade, tradespeople adding drywall to their skills and contractors training new crew members.

[WHAT IS INCLUDED: tools, materials, safety equipment, certificate of completion]

How registration works

Seats are limited for each session so every participant gets time on the wall.

  1. Choose a session

    Pick a course and a date with seats available.

  2. Register online

    Fill in the registration form. It takes about two minutes.

  3. Confirm your seat

    We contact you to confirm your seat and payment.

  4. Attend the course

    Arrive with work clothes and closed shoes. We supply the rest.

Courses and dates

Prices are per participant. For group or company training, contact us.
